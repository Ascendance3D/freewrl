## 2026-11-06 - Deprecated SourceForge URL
**Learning:** The legacy URL `http://freewrl.sf.net` is outdated and requires two HTTP 301 redirects to reach `https://freewrl.sourceforge.io/`.
**Action:** Replace any remaining instances of `http://freewrl.sf.net` with `https://freewrl.sourceforge.io/` when modifying documentation or user-facing strings.
