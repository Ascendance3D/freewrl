## 2024-10-31 - [CLI Formatting]
**Learning:** `freewrl --help` output contained many small capitalization, grammar, and alignment inconsistencies due to its lengthy list of ad-hoc options.
**Action:** When updating or reviewing `--help` text, ensure descriptions start with an uppercase letter, end with a period where appropriate, correctly format enumerations as comma-separated lists, and capitalize common acronyms (e.g., IPv4, PDU) for consistency.
